Conclave

An invite-only gathering for Presidents, Vice Presidents, Directors and Coordinators from economics, business, consulting and finance societies across Delhi NCR, run in collaboration with ABC. Built so student leaders can compare notes with people carrying the same weight elsewhere.

4th February 2024 · Delhi NCR
Conclave '24, with the Ashoka Business Club

The inaugural Leadership Conclave was jointly organised with the Ashoka Business Club as a precursor to Equilibrium 2.0. It gave leaders of clubs and societies across economics, business, entrepreneurship, consulting and finance a shared platform, drawing 65+ delegates from prominent colleges across Delhi NCR for a day of insightful dialogue and on-the-spot networking that sparked long-term connections and collaborations.

2nd February 2025 · Delhi
Conclave '25, with the Ashoka Business Club

The second edition, again jointly hosted with the Ashoka Business Club, grew the room to 80+ representatives from more than 30 colleges and societies across Delhi NCR and beyond. Delegates spoke on their own society's vision and past work, sparking fresh conversation around collaboration and knowledge-sharing between the leaders driving these communities forward.

Olympiad

Open to grades 10 to 12 across India, run entirely online through Unstop: a 40-question application quiz, then group discussion rounds that narrow the field to a final podium. Winners take home a full ride to Ashoka's Young Scholar Programme or Ashoka Horizons.

Seminars

The Seminars team plans and runs talks, book launches and webinars at Ashoka, from inviting the speaker through to moderating the session on the day. Some of the people who have joined us:

Dr. Arvind Virmani
Hon'ble Member, NITI Aayog

Joined AUES for a seminar on India's development policy, from inclusive growth models to the road to Viksit Bharat.

Monika Halan
Author & Speaker

Spoke on "Let's Talk Money: The Earning Years", on building good money habits early in a career.

Prof. S. Mahendra Dev
Chairman, EAC-PM

Chairman of the Economic Advisory Council to the Prime Minister, on how research travels from the desk to policy.

Duvvuri Subbarao
Former Governor, RBI

The economist and former RBI Governor spoke on the dilemmas of central banking, from managing inflation to monetary policy trade-offs.

Gurucharan Das
Author & former CEO, P&G India

The author and former CEO of Procter & Gamble India joined students for a session on economics, ideas, and public life.
