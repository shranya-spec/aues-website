AUER

Ashoka's first peer-reviewed undergraduate economics journal. Founded in 2020, run entirely by students, and built to give undergraduate research a real place to be published.

Founded 2020 · Currently on Volume 4

Peer-reviewed, student-run, and open to more than economics majors.

The AUER is managed by the Ashoka University Economics Society and seeks out novel, insightful undergraduate research. It accepts research papers, commentaries and articles, and book reviews, and actively encourages submissions with a developing-world lens.

Articles are received on a rolling annual basis, and a selection is published each year in a single volume. Every volume needs a minimum of five accepted pieces to go to print.

Macroeconomic Policy Inequality & Growth Political Science Financial Markets Health & Education Business Statistics Environmental Economics Technology & Innovation Economic History Microeconomics

What we publish

Research papers, commentaries and articles, and book reviews, on any of the topics above or related fields.

How it works

Submissions are accepted year-round. Selected pieces are compiled into a single volume once a year, minimum five articles.

Get in touch

Email auer@ashoka.edu.in with submissions or questions about the review.
