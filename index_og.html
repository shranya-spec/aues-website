Who we are

A society for people who keep asking why.

The Ashoka University Economics Society aims to actively contribute to the dialogue around economics in Ashoka. As an ever-evolving and dynamic subject, economics plays an important role in the lives of all individuals.

We take economics beyond the classroom by providing students with an intellectually stimulating environment which allows them to engage with and critically think and talk about the issues gripping the world.

That work runs through competitions, seminars, mentorship programmes and Equilibrium, our flagship economics festival. Under the same roof sit the Ashoka University Economic Review, Ashoka's first peer-reviewed undergraduate economics journal, and The Gazette, our student-run publication.

Our vision is to make the subject more accessible, and to inspire students to engage with ideas beyond the confines of their courses.
