What we are

The Ashoka University Economics Society contributes to the dialogue around economics at Ashoka. We take the subject beyond the classroom, giving students an environment to engage critically with the issues gripping the world.

That work runs through competitions, seminars, mentorship, and Equilibrium, our flagship festival. Under the same roof sit the Ashoka University Economic Review, Ashoka's first peer-reviewed undergraduate economics journal, and The Gazette, our student-run publication.

Our vision is to make the subject more accessible, and to inspire students to engage with ideas beyond the confines of their courses.
