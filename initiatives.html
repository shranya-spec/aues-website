Conclave

An invite-only gathering for Presidents, Vice Presidents, Directors and Coordinators from economics, business, consulting and finance societies across Delhi NCR, run in collaboration with the Ashoka Business Club.

4th February 2024 · Delhi NCR
Conclave '24

The inaugural Leadership Conclave was jointly organised with the Ashoka Business Club as a precursor to Equilibrium 2.0. It gave leaders of clubs and societies across economics, business, entrepreneurship, consulting and finance a shared platform, drawing 65+ delegates from prominent colleges across Delhi NCR for a day of insightful dialogue and on-the-spot networking that sparked long-term connections and collaborations.

65+Delegates
1stEdition
2nd February 2025 · Delhi
Conclave '25

The second edition, again jointly hosted with the Ashoka Business Club, grew the room to 80+ representatives from more than 30 colleges and societies across Delhi NCR and beyond. Delegates spoke on their own society's vision and past work, sparking fresh conversation around collaboration and knowledge-sharing between the leaders driving these communities forward.

Olympiad

Open to grades 10 to 12 across India, run entirely online through Unstop: a 40-question application quiz, then group discussion rounds that narrow the field to a final podium. Winners take home a full ride to Ashoka's Young Scholar Programme or Ashoka Horizons.
