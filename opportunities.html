Campus Growth Associate, with Zyber

AUES's first live project of the year: helping Zyber, an AI-native social networking platform, launch and grow at Ashoka.

Zyber connects students with the right person, for the right reason, at the right time. It's already being built across leading universities in India, with 500+ student builders contributing to its campus growth.

As a Campus Growth Associate, you'd join Zyber's growth team and work directly with the Founder, driving adoption at Ashoka through growth strategy, user acquisition, events and product feedback.

8–12 weeksDuration
6–10 hrs/weekCommitment

What you'd get out of it

Hands-on exposure to business development, growth strategy, startup operations and AI-first workflows, working directly alongside a founder rather than through a case study.
